1. RACE OVERVIEW – MAY 23RD & 24TH, 2026

The Sulphur Springs Trail Race weaves its way along a 20K trail loop through the beautiful Dundas Valley Conservation Area in Hamilton, Ontario. With 520m of elevation gain per 20K loop, the grind is real, and the views are worth it. This race weekend offers events for everyone, from the weekend warrior to the hard‑core racer, with multiple relay opportunities for the corporate team looking for a challenge:

10K • 20K • 50K • 50 Miles • 100K • 100 Miles  
50 Mile Night Relay (up to 4 runners)  
100 Mile Team Relay (up to 8 runners)

Race distances are all based on a multiple of the 20K loop, and runners return to the Start/Finish area after each loop, creating an incredible atmosphere for the runners, support crew and spectators alike.

3. SULPHUR SPRINGS TRAIL RACE RULES

Violation of any race rules or directives may be grounds for disqualification from this race as well as future Straw Dog races. Any inquiries that you have will be directed to Race Management, and all decisions made by the Race Management are final.

There will be NO unofficial runners. Registered runners and registered Pacers only.

The official race bib contains a timing chip and must be worn on your front while racing. Relay runners will ALSO use an ankle strap to pass to the next runner.

Runners must follow the marked trail at all times. Any runner departing from the official trail must return unassisted to the point of departure prior to continuing.

Each runner must complete the course under their own power.

Poles are only permitted for the 50 Mile, 100K & 100 Mile solo runners.

Earphones and music players are heavily discouraged.

Runners may not accept assistance in ANY FORM from anyone between aid stations.

Runners may not store supplies of any kind along the trail.

All runners are responsible for the actions of their support crew & pacers.

Doping during the event will result in disqualification.

Littering of any kind is grounds for disqualification.

Runners can be disqualified for any act of bad sportsmanship.

We all rely on volunteers — please respect and thank the volunteers.

Pacers may accompany runners under the guidelines shown in the PACERS section.

Runners & pacers on the trail at night must have no less than 2 light sources.

16. THE TRAIL (AND AFTER DARK)

The Dundas Valley trail system includes:

Rolling single‑track

Double‑track rail trail

Wooden bridges

Rooted and rocky sections

Open meadows and shaded forest

Trail conditions can vary depending on weather.
Expect mud, slippery roots, and uneven terrain after rainfall.

Night Running Requirements:

All runners and pacers on the trail after dark must carry two light sources  
(e.g., headlamp + handheld, or two headlamps).

Reflective markers and glow sticks will be placed along the course.

Be aware of wildlife — including deer, raccoons, and coyotes.

Trail Etiquette:

Stay to the right; pass on the left.

Announce your pass (“On your left”).

Be courteous to other runners and hikers.

No littering — pack out everything.

20. PACERS

Pacers are allowed for the following events:

50 Mile (solo)

100K (solo)

100 Mile (solo)

100 Mile Team Relay

Pacer Rules:

Pacers may join their runner only after dark or at the designated point announced by Race Management.

Pacers must check in at the Start/Finish area and receive a pacer bib.

Only one pacer may accompany a runner at a time.

Pacers may not carry supplies, food, or water for their runner.

Pacers must follow all race rules, including trail etiquette and lighting requirements.

Pacers must stay with their runner at all times and may not run ahead to “scout” the trail.

Pacers must have two light sources when pacing at night.

Pacers are there for:

Safety

Navigation support

Moral encouragement

Monitoring runner condition

Pacers may not provide physical assistance (pushing, pulling, carrying gear).

22. WESTERN STATES ENDURANCE RUN QUALIFICATION

The 100 Mile event at Sulphur Springs Trail Race is an official Western States Endurance Run (WSER) Qualifier.

To qualify:

You must complete the 100 Mile race

You must finish within the official cutoff time

Your name will be submitted to WSER organizers after results are verified

Being a WSER qualifier means your finish earns you one ticket into the Western States lottery, increasing your chances of selection.

NOTES:

Only the 100 Mile solo event is a qualifier

Relay teams do not qualify

No other distances qualify

WSER rules and lottery details are available on the WSER website
